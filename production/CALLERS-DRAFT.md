# K-JAM new callers (DRAFT v2, not recorded yet)

Nine new callers, none about traffic. Each one is a small scene that escalates and ends on a twist (about 35–50 s). Mark anything to cut or rewrite.
Numbers are written as digits here and get spelled out for the voices when recorded. Lines in *(italics)* are delivery notes, not spoken.

**Voices:** the four existing caller voices are reused under new names (two women, two men; the phone filter helps them sound different). Rosa needs one new voice, an older woman, picked from the ElevenLabs Voice Library.

---

**call-05 · Dale, the metal detector guy** (CALLER-M)
- RICK: Caller, you're on K-JAM.
- DALE: Rick! Dale. I'm down on the beach with my metal detector.
- DANA: Ooh! Find any treasure?
- DALE: Today? Four car keys, two wedding rings, and somebody's retainer.
- DANA: Those poor people! What are you going to do with the rings?
- DALE: Well, one of them's mine.
- DANA: Oh, you found yours! Your wife will be so happy!
- DALE: She's the one who threw it in the ocean. In 2019.
- RICK: Dale. Put the ring back in the sand.
- DALE: I have. Four times. It keeps finding me, Rick.
- DANA: And the retainer?
- DALE: *(talking through a retainer)* Fits great.

**call-06 · Cheryl, the psychic** (CALLER-F)
- DANA: Line one, you're on K-JAM!
- CHERYL: Don't tell me your names. I'm a psychic.
- RICK: Ma'am, we've been saying our names on the radio for three hours.
- CHERYL: I'm sensing... a Rick.
- RICK: Yes.
- CHERYL: And a... Diana.
- DANA: So close!
- CHERYL: Rick. The spirits have a message. Someone from your past is trying to reach you.
- RICK: It's my dentist.
- CHERYL: They say it's been a long time.
- RICK: Six years.
- CHERYL: They say the tooth is not going to fix itself.
- DANA: Rick! The spirits are right! Go to the dentist!
- CHERYL: That'll be forty dollars.

**call-07 · Doug, the ninth caller** (CALLER-M)
- DANA: You're on K-JAM!
- DOUG: Yes! Am I the ninth caller?
- DANA: Oh, sweetie, we're not running a contest.
- DOUG: I know. I've been calling since Tuesday. Every time, I'm the ninth.
- RICK: How are you always the ninth?
- DOUG: I call eight times first and hang up on myself.
- DANA: Aww! That's dedication!
- RICK: That's a cry for help.
- DANA: You know what, Doug? You win!
- DOUG: What do I win?
- DANA: You win... the sound of Rick sighing!
- RICK: *(a long, tired sigh)*
- DOUG: *(choked up)* I'm going to frame it.

**call-08 · Tasha, who named a cactus after Rick** (CALLER-F)
- DANA: Caller, go ahead!
- TASHA: Hi! I just wanted Rick to know I named my cactus after him.
- DANA: Aww!
- RICK: Why.
- TASHA: He's prickly, he doesn't like to be touched, and he hasn't moved from the same corner in six years.
- RICK: ...Fair.
- TASHA: I also named my golden retriever Dana.
- DANA: Oh, I love that! Why?
- TASHA: She's excited about everything, and she keeps knocking Rick over.
- RICK: Also fair.
- TASHA: But last week, Rick bloomed! One little pink flower. It lasted twenty minutes.
- DANA: Rick! That's you at the holiday party!
- RICK: We agreed. We never talk about the holiday party.

**call-09 · Linda and the pelican** (CALLER-F)
- RICK: Caller, you're on the air.
- LINDA: Hi. A pelican has been sitting on my balcony for three days. I named him Steven.
- RICK: Linda, you can't keep a pelican.
- LINDA: I'm not keeping him. He's keeping me. He ate my sandwich this morning. And the plate.
- DANA: The plate?!
- LINDA: Then he brought friends. There are six of them now. They take shifts. Steven does mornings.
- DANA: Aww, a little pelican family!
- LINDA: Today I came home and they'd changed the locks.
- RICK: Linda, where are you calling from right now?
- LINDA: My car. Steven has the apartment.
- RICK: Steven has a better place than me.

**call-10 · Rosa, Rick's biggest fan** (CALLER-OLD, a new voice)
- DANA: Rosa! You're on K-JAM!
- ROSA: Hello, dear. I'm calling for Rick.
- RICK: This is Rick.
- ROSA: Oh! Rick. I've listened to you for thirty years. You have the most soothing voice.
- RICK: Well. Thank you, Rosa.
- ROSA: Like a dishwasher finishing its cycle.
- DANA: That's the nicest thing anyone's ever said about Rick!
- RICK: It's the only thing anyone's ever said about Rick.
- ROSA: Are you single, dear?
- DANA: *(gasps)* He is!
- RICK: Dana.
- ROSA: I make a very good lasagna. And I still have my license.
- RICK: We have to go to a song.
- ROSA: That's fine, dear. I'll hold.

**call-11 · Marcus, four years of surf lessons** (CALLER-M)
- DANA: Line two! You're on K-JAM!
- MARCUS: Hey! Marcus here. Just finished my surf lesson.
- DANA: Stoked! Did you stand up?
- MARCUS: No. That's year four of not standing up.
- RICK: Four years.
- MARCUS: My instructor stopped trying. Now we just float out there and talk about our feelings.
- DANA: That's kind of beautiful, actually.
- MARCUS: He's going through a divorce. Last week he cried so hard he caught a wave.
- RICK: Marcus, you're not taking surf lessons. You're his therapist.
- MARCUS: ...He does pay me now. Is that weird?
- RICK: Charge him more.

**call-12 · June and the garage sale** (CALLER-F, then HAL on CALLER-M)
- RICK: Go ahead, caller.
- JUNE: Hi! Garage sale tomorrow, 8 a.m., everybody come!
- DANA: Ooh! What've you got?
- JUNE: A bread maker, never used. A treadmill, never used. A guitar, never used.
- RICK: I see a pattern, June.
- JUNE: And my husband's golf clubs.
- DANA: Oh, he doesn't golf?
- JUNE: He golfs every weekend. He just doesn't know they're for sale yet.
- RICK: Hold on, June. We've got Hal on line two.
- HAL: June. I'm in the car. I'm listening.
- JUNE: *(beat)* ...Everything's half off!

**call-13 · Dana's Cousin Kevin** (CALLER-M; a callback to his band, which K-JAM plays)
- DANA: Oh no. Rick, it's Kevin.
- RICK: Which Kevin?
- KEVIN: Cousin Kevin! Hey cuz! Did you play my song yet?
- DANA: We played it an hour ago!
- KEVIN: I missed it. I was in the shower. Can you play it again? And can you say it's number one?
- RICK: It's not number one, Kevin.
- KEVIN: Can you say it's number one somewhere?
- RICK: *(sighs)* Bonfire on the Beach is the number one song by Dana's Cousin Kevin.
- KEVIN: *(away from the phone)* MOM! I'M NUMBER ONE!
