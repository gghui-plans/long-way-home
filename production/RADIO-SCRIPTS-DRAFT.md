## 1. Fast Lane Facts (new recurring segment)

The idea is a real, checkable fact that Dana loves and Rick turns into traffic misery. Facts are California, coast and road themed.
Episodes play in order and are remembered between drives, so nobody hears #1 twice before hearing #16.
The segment is about 20–30 s, roughly once every 4–5 breaks.

**facts-01 · The Sig Alert**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! Rick, did you know the Sig Alert is named after a real person?
- RICK: Loyd Sigmon. Radio man. 1955. He built a machine so the police could warn every station about traffic at once.
- DANA: What a legacy!
- RICK: Seventy years of warnings, Dana. Not one of them has fixed anything.

**facts-02 · The traffic cone**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! The traffic cone was invented right here in L.A. A city street painter named Charles Scanlon made the first one in 1940, out of old tires!
- RICK: So it's a local. That explains the attitude.
- DANA: He was tired of cars running over his wooden signs!
- RICK: And now the cones are tired of us. Full circle.

**facts-03 · Cruise control**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! Cruise control was invented by Ralph Teetor, an engineer who was blind.
- RICK: The story goes, he rode with a lawyer who sped up when he listened and slowed down when he talked.
- DANA: So he invented a whole machine to fix one bad driver!
- RICK: A man who couldn't see the road, Dana. Still drove better than everyone on the 405.

**facts-04 · The turn signal**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! The turn signal was invented by a silent movie star! Florence Lawrence, 1914. You pushed a button and a little flag popped up on the back bumper.
- RICK: And she never patented it. She just gave it to the world.
- DANA: That's so generous!
- RICK: And the world said, no thank you. Most ignored invention in California.
- DANA: Somebody uses it! That minivan! Since breakfast!

**facts-05 · The avocado**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! Every Hass avocado on Earth traces back to one single tree, planted by a mailman named Rudolph Hass in La Habra Heights!
- RICK: One tree.
- DANA: One tree!
- RICK: And it's still two dollars extra.

**facts-06 · Los Angeles is moving**
- RICK: Fast Lane Facts. Los Angeles is sliding toward San Francisco. About 2 inches a year. San Andreas Fault.
- DANA: We're moving? Without packing?
- RICK: In about 15 million years, we'll be neighbors.
- DANA: So the drive up there is getting shorter!
- RICK: The only commute in California that gets shorter every year, Dana. And it's still faster than the 5.

**facts-07 · Pelicans**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! Brown pelicans dive headfirst into the ocean from 60 feet up!
- RICK: Headfirst. On purpose.
- DANA: They've got little air sacs under their skin. Like built-in bubble wrap!
- RICK: So the pelican has better safety features than my car.

**facts-08 · Sea otters**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! Sea otters hold hands while they sleep, so they don't drift apart.
- RICK: Okay. That's nice. I'll allow it.
- DANA: And they have the thickest fur of any animal. Up to a million hairs per square inch!
- RICK: Warm coat, floating on their back, holding hands at sunset. The otter figured out life, Dana. I'm on the 10.

**facts-09 · Grunion**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! On spring and summer nights, little silver fish called grunion wiggle right up onto California beaches to lay their eggs in the sand!
- RICK: Fish. On the beach. On purpose.
- DANA: Scientists predict the nights from the tides, and people come out with flashlights to watch!
- RICK: It's called a grunion run. The only thing in L.A. that shows up exactly on schedule.

**facts-10 · The car radio**
- RICK: Fast Lane Facts. Motorola. Motor, plus Victrola. It started out in 1930 as a radio for your car.
- DANA: So car radio is where we come from!
- RICK: Ninety-five years of car radio. And it all led to this.
- DANA: To us!
- RICK: I'm sorry, everyone.

**facts-11 · The green flash**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! Watch the ocean right when the sun goes down. Sometimes the last little sliver flashes green!
- RICK: That's real. It's called the green flash. The air bends the light like a prism.
- DANA: Rick! You know something nice!
- RICK: Don't tell anyone. Also, don't stare at the sun while driving. That's also a fact.

**facts-12 · The Pacific**
- RICK: Fast Lane Facts. The explorer Magellan named this ocean the Pacific. It means peaceful.
- DANA: Aww, it is peaceful!
- RICK: He never saw a beach parking lot on a Saturday.

**facts-13 · Highway 1 at Big Sur**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! The Big Sur stretch of Highway 1 took 18 years to build. It finally opened in 1937!
- RICK: Eighteen years. For one road.
- DANA: Through cliffs, over canyons, right along the ocean!
- RICK: Still faster than the lane closure by my house.

**facts-14 · Gray whales**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! Gray whales swim from Alaska down to Mexico and back every year. About 10,000 miles round trip!
- RICK: And never once ask for directions.
- DANA: They just follow the coast. Like us!
- RICK: They're in the fast lane, Dana. We're behind Gary.

**facts-15 · There's no such bird as a seagull**
- RICK: Fast Lane Facts. There is no such bird as a seagull.
- DANA: What? I see them every day!
- RICK: Those are gulls. Western gulls, California gulls, Heermann's gulls. Lots of kinds. None of them are called seagull.
- DANA: Then who's been stealing my french fries?
- RICK: A Western gull, Dana. And he knows your car.

**facts-16 · Hollywoodland**
- DANA: Fast Lane Facts! The Hollywood sign used to say Hollywoodland. It went up in 1923 as an ad for new houses!
- RICK: The most famous landmark in L.A. is a billboard.
- DANA: They took off the "land" in 1949!
- RICK: Even the sign couldn't afford land in L.A.

**Spare facts** (swap in for any you don't like):
- Giant kelp off the coast can grow about 2 feet in a day. RICK: "Faster than the 405."
- Windshield wipers were invented by Mary Anderson in 1903, after she watched a streetcar driver lean out in the snow. RICK: "People said it would distract drivers. Then it rained."
- George Freeth showed Californians surfing in 1907, as a stunt to sell train tickets to the beach. RICK: "The first surf trip was a marketing campaign. Nothing's changed."
- The California poppy closes up at night and on cloudy days. RICK: "Same, poppy. Same."
- A hummingbird's heart can beat over 1,200 times a minute. RICK: "That's me at a four-way stop."

## 3. Things that react to what's happening (new code hooks)

These make the station feel live. Each one fires only when its moment happens, so they rarely repeat.

**Real clock** (on the first break of a drive, chosen by your device's time)
- clock-morning · DANA: Good morning, coast! It's sunset here at K-JAM. It's always sunset here at K-JAM. / RICK: We don't know how it happened. We stopped asking.
- clock-midday · RICK: If you're driving with us on your lunch break, a word of advice. The fries go under the seat, and they never come back. / DANA: Never! It's a whole ecosystem down there!
- clock-evening · DANA: Evening drive time, folks! The real sunset and our sunset, at the same time! / RICK: Twice the squinting.
- clock-late · RICK: If you're driving with us this late, you're either very relaxed or very lost. / DANA: Or both! Welcome!

**You started steering** (after you pick a few turns yourself)
- steer-01 · RICK: Oh, look who's steering now. / DANA: Ooh, a scenic detour! I love it!
- steer-02 · DANA: Somebody's taking the scenic route! / RICK: Every route is scenic out there, Dana. That's the problem. Nobody can decide.

**Cruise speed**
- speed-fast · RICK: Somebody just maxed out the cruise control. / DANA: The ocean's not going anywhere! / RICK: Neither is the Highway Patrol.
- speed-slow · DANA: Somebody's taking it nice and slow out there. Love that. / RICK: Gary approves.

**Postcard taken**
- postcard-01 · DANA: Did somebody just snap a postcard? Say cheese, coast! / RICK: The Corvette doesn't have a bad side. It knows it.
- postcard-02 · RICK: Another postcard. Send one to the 405. Let them see what they're missing.

**Pier or Ferris wheel coming up**
- pier-01 · DANA: Ferris wheel up ahead! Give it a wave! / RICK: It's been going in circles all day and it's having more fun than the 10.
- pier-02 · RICK: Pier coming up. Somebody's fishing off the end. Nobody has ever caught anything. They're there for the peace and quiet. / DANA: Aww. / RICK: Then we drive by playing Sig Alert.

## 4. Optional: Dana's Traffic-scopes (recurring, pure comedy, no facts)

**scope-01**
- DANA: Dana's Traffic-scopes! Leo, today you'll find the perfect parking spot. Then you'll drive past it because there might be a better one.
- RICK: There won't be.
- DANA: Pisces, go with the flow!
- RICK: The flow is stopped, Pisces. The flow has been stopped since Tuesday.

**scope-02**
- DANA: Traffic-scopes! Gemini, you'll be torn between two lanes!
- RICK: Both lanes are bad, Gemini. Pick one and live with it.
- DANA: Capricorn, a stranger will let you merge!
- RICK: It won't happen, Capricorn. I don't want you getting your hopes up.

**scope-03**
- DANA: Traffic-scopes! Aries, a green light is in your future!
- RICK: Then a red one. Then another red one. That's how lights work, Aries.
- DANA: Virgo, today you'll finally clean out the car!
- RICK: You'll find a fry from 2019. Leave it. It's earned its place.

**scope-04**
- DANA: Traffic-scopes! Scorpio, someone is thinking about you!
- RICK: It's the person behind you, Scorpio. They are thinking about you very much. Use your signal.
- DANA: Sagittarius, adventure calls!
- RICK: It's the dashboard light, Sagittarius. Get it checked.
