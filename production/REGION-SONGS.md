## Redwood Coast (89.3 The Coffee Cabin)

Sound: 2000s–2010s acoustic pop and indie folk. Warm acoustic guitar, clean modern production, sincere vocals. Themes: happy and sincere: love, family, nostalgia, hometown. No heartbreak. Marj and Walt carry the comedy; the songs carry the feeling.

Suno won't accept artist names, so each Style of Music describes the sound instead.

- **Duration:** Custom, 2:00 to 2:30
- **Weirdness:** about 30%
- **Style Influence:** about 70%
- **Variety:** Normal
