## Redwood Coast

Sound: acoustic folk and Americana. Warm, unhurried, campfire feel. A big contrast with K-JAM's rock.

### Settings for every Redwood Coast song (set once)

- **Mode:** Custom
- **Exclude styles:** modern pop, EDM, trap, autotune, lo-fi, distorted electric guitar, heavy drums
- **Duration:** Custom, 2:00 to 2:30
- **Weirdness:** about 30%
- **Style Influence:** about 70%
- **Variety:** Normal
- **Personalize:** Off

### 1. Fog Line (test song)

- **Save as:** fog-line.mp3
- **Title:** Fog Line
- **Band (for the radio):** The Fogbank Family Band
- **Style of Music:** 1970s acoustic folk, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, upright bass, soft brushed drums, harmonica, gentle harmonies, warm female vocals, unhurried, campfire warmth, analog recording
- **Vocal Gender:** Female
- **Theme:** leaving the city for the redwoods. A "fog line" is the white line along the road's edge, so the title is about both the drive and the weather. "Radio fading" nods to the K-JAM signal handoff.
