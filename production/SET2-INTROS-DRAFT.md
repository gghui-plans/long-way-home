# K-JAM set 2: song intros (approved 2026-10-27, now in PRODUCTION-PACK.md Part 1)

**The pack is the final version.** After the Couples Skate test, Rick sounded flat: three "dry/deadpan/flat" tags in a row at stability 0.75 left his pitch barely moving. His story lines got livelier directions in the pack ("caught off guard, then fond", "dry, building, a little proud"), and a `[pause]` now goes after the direction instead of being the direction. The user picked that take (take 2).

Two intros for each of the 14 set 2 songs, written 2026-10-27:

- **Short intro** (`intro-…`, about 3–8 s): one host, the band, the title, one quick joke.
- **Story intro** (`introb-…`, about 15–25 s): Rick and Dana's take on Marj and Walt's story intros. Each one is a host's summer memory of the place in the song, so it fits the set's theme, "Postcards from one summer". The game alternates the two intros and prefers the one you haven't heard.

About 5,500 characters in all: 1,100 for the short intros and 4,400 for the stories (15–25 s each). Budget about 6,000–7,000 ElevenLabs credits with retakes. Once approved, the rows move into PRODUCTION-PACK.md Part 1, with story lines as `introb-<song>-1`, `-2` and so on, and then generate-voices.ps1 makes them. Mark each one keep, cut or change.

## Rick and Dana: lore sheet

The facts the stories share. The first group of each list is already on air, so don't contradict it.

**Rick**
- On air already: 30 years of traffic reports. Deadpan, weary, a stickler for signaling and merging. Won't dance (Dana, on the Cabin call). Secretly soft: he cried at Marj and Walt's call and said "It's allergies". Tried surfing once, and the ocean won. Six years since he saw a dentist. His friend Gary "always signaled", then moved north.
- New: a teenager in L.A. at the turn of the 80s. His mother's station wagon was his first car. One couples skate, in the summer of '79, with Debbie Pruitt (they held the wall; she married the DJ). Drove an ice cream truck in the summer of '82. Got into radio because of a traffic reporter he heard as a kid. A taco truck guy, Hector, has fed him every morning before the show for years. Keeps half a photo-booth strip in his sun visor; Gary has the other half.
- **Rule:** Rick's stories start dry and turn out to be sweet, and he never admits a song or a memory got to him. He changes the subject or reads the band name.

**Dana**
- On air already: bright, quick, an optimist. Dana's shortcut of the day. Her cousin Kevin has a band (Dana's Cousin Kevin), calls the station, lives with his mom, and married a woman he "met at a merge" ("She let me in").
- New: grew up on a cul-de-sac in a big, loud family: her dad, her mom, three brothers, and Kevin always around. Her dad runs the grill at every block party in a hot-dog hat, and on road trips he picks motels by their pool. Her first date was at a drive-in, top down, in the rain. The summer she was sixteen, she worked at the boardwalk arcade. She sneaks up to the station roof to watch the sunset.
- **Rule:** Dana's stories get out of hand, usually thanks to Kevin. She always finds the sweet part, and she's the one who catches Rick being soft.

**Kevin**, never heard in these (he only calls in): the "merge" where he met his wife was two people going for the same dryer at a laundromat.

## Story intros (introb-*)

### 17. Top Down, Radio Up · What a job
- DANA: [bright, curious] Rick, what did you do on the first day of summer when you were a kid?
- RICK: [dry] Washed my mother's station wagon.
- DANA: [laughing] That's it?
- RICK: [slow, remembering] Then I drove it to the beach. Windows down, radio up. Some guy on the radio telling everybody where the traffic was. I thought, what a job.
- DANA: [touched] Aww! And now that guy is you!
- RICK: [flat] Now that guy is me. Nobody listens to him either.
- DANA: [bright] Rush Hour Rebels! Top Down, Radio Up!

### 18. Ice Cream Truck · The fast one
- RICK: [grave] Summer of eighty-two, I drove an ice cream truck.
- DANA: [gasps, delighted] Rick! You were the ice cream man?
- RICK: [haunted] Eight hours a day. Same song. I still hear it at red lights.
- DANA: [dreamy] The kids must have loved you.
- RICK: [slow, almost fond] Whole neighborhoods running after me. Only time in my life anybody was happy to see me in traffic.
- DANA: [warm, laughing] The Carpoolers, Ice Cream Truck!

### 19. Couples Skate · The wall
- DANA: [teasing] Rick. Be honest. Did you ever couples skate?
- RICK: [pause] [dry] Once. Summer of seventy-nine. Debbie Pruitt.
- DANA: [gasps, delighted] Rick! How did it go?
- RICK: [deadpan] I held the wall. She held the wall. We held the wall together for three songs.
- DANA: [touched] That's actually really sweet.
- RICK: [flat] She married the DJ.
- DANA: [laughing] The Turn Signals, Couples Skate!

### 20. Motel Pool · The cannonball
- DANA: [nostalgic, bright] Every summer, my dad drove us up the coast, and he picked the motel by the pool.
- RICK: [dry] Not the room.
- DANA: [cheerful] Never the room! One year the pool was the size of a hot tub. Four of us kids in it. Plus Kevin.
- RICK: [weary] Kevin was there.
- DANA: [proud] Kevin did a cannonball. Emptied it.
- RICK: [deadpan] The whole pool.
- DANA: [bright] Most of it! Tailgate Thunder, Motel Pool!

### 22. Drive-In Double Feature · Both movies
- DANA: [dreamy] My first date was at a drive-in. He had a convertible.
- RICK: [suspicious] Top up or down?
- DANA: [laughing] Down! It started raining halfway through the first movie.
- RICK: [dry] So you left.
- DANA: [proud] We stayed for both! Under a beach umbrella. I couldn't tell you one thing about either movie.
- RICK: [deadpan] That's not romance, Dana. That's an upholstery claim.
- DANA: [laughing] Cruise Control, Drive-In Double Feature!

### 23. Taco Truck Moon · Drive safe
- RICK: [quiet] Thirty years I've come in before sunrise. There's a taco truck on the corner by the station.
- DANA: [warm] Hector!
- RICK: [fond] Hector. Every morning, four o'clock, two tacos. And he always says, "Drive safe, Rick."
- DANA: [touched] That's so sweet.
- RICK: [dry] I'm not driving, Hector. I'm parked on a freeway. It's different.
- DANA: [whispering, conspiratorial] He listens every morning, you know. [bright] Hector, this one's for you! The Parking Meters, Taco Truck Moon!

### 24. High Score · Practice
- DANA: [excited] The summer I was sixteen, I worked at the boardwalk arcade. I had the high score on every machine. D-A-N.
- RICK: [suspicious] You worked there.
- DANA: [proud] Yep!
- RICK: [dry] You had the keys.
- DANA: [pause] [guilty] ...Free play is still playing.
- RICK: [flat] Hazard Lights. High Score. Dana cheated.
- DANA: [protesting] I practiced!

### 25. Laundromat Love · The merge
- DANA: [excited] Kevin says Laundromat Love is about how he met his wife.
- RICK: [suspicious] He told us they met at a merge.
- DANA: [explaining] At the laundromat! One dryer left, and they both went for it.
- RICK: [dry] That's not a merge, Dana. That's a collision.
- DANA: [dreamy] She let him have it. Then she folded his socks.
- RICK: [pause] [grudging] ...Okay. That's a merge. Dana's Cousin Kevin, Laundromat Love.

### 26. Fireworks on the Water · Traffic was terrible
- RICK: [quiet] One Fourth of July, I did the traffic from the helicopter.
- DANA: [gasps] You hate flying!
- RICK: [dry] I hate most things, Dana. Keep up. [pause] [slow, quiet] Nine o'clock, every town on the coast went off at once. Twenty shows, all the way down the water.
- DANA: [softly] Rick... that sounds beautiful.
- RICK: [clears throat] [gruff] Traffic was terrible. The Fender Benders. Fireworks on the Water.

### 27. Photo Booth · The other half
- DANA: [sly] Rick. What's the photo-booth strip in your sun visor?
- RICK: [too quickly] Nothing.
- DANA: [teasing] Four pictures! And you're smiling!
- RICK: [defensive] That's not smiling. The flash went off early.
- DANA: [gently] It's torn down the middle. Who has the other half?
- RICK: [pause] [quiet] Gary.
- DANA: [touched] Gary who moved?
- RICK: [gruff] Gary always signaled. The Turn Signals. Photo Booth.

### 28. Block Party · The speech
- DANA: [excited] My street had a block party every summer. My dad ran the grill in a hat shaped like a hot dog.
- RICK: [dry] Of course he did.
- DANA: [warm] And every year he made the same speech. "Look around. These are your neighbors."
- RICK: [pause] [quietly] ...I don't know my neighbors.
- DANA: [gently] Rick.
- RICK: [defensive] One of them waves. I wave back. We're very close.
- DANA: [bright] Bring a chair, Rick! Gridlock Prophets, Block Party!

### 29. One-Hour Sailboat · Six hours
- DANA: [excited] Kevin and I rented a little sailboat once. One hour.
- RICK: [dry] Neither of you can sail.
- DANA: [cheerful] Nope! We drifted out past the harbor, and the wind just... stopped.
- RICK: [concerned] How long were you out there?
- DANA: [breezy] Six hours! Kevin wrote three songs.
- RICK: [deadpan] Six hours, not moving, nowhere to go. Dana, that's my commute with a sail.
- DANA: [laughing] Valet Overdrive, One-Hour Sailboat!

### 31. Rooftop at Dusk · Seven fifteen
- DANA: [hushed] Confession. Some nights after the show, I go up on the station roof to watch the sunset.
- RICK: [dry] That door says authorized personnel only.
- DANA: [cheerful] I'm personnel!
- RICK: [suspicious] Are you authorized?
- DANA: [dreamy, ignoring him] The whole city goes orange. Then the brake lights come on, all the way to the ocean, like a big red river.
- RICK: [pause] [quietly] ...What time?
- DANA: [delighted] Seven fifteen. Cruise Control, Rooftop at Dusk.

### 32. Last Night of Summer · All the windows
- DANA: [wistful] Rick, what does the last night of summer mean to you?
- RICK: [dry] Tuesday.
- DANA: [laughing] Come on.
- RICK: [pause] [quiet] My mother let me take the station wagon out one last time. I'd drive the coast with the top down.
- DANA: [confused] Rick, a station wagon doesn't have a top.
- RICK: [softly] I rolled all the windows down and pretended. [pause] [gruff] Don't look at me like that.
- DANA: [tender] The Turn Signals. Last Night of Summer.
