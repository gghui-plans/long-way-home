# K-JAM voice lines, grouped by voice

Every line the radio needs, sorted by who says it, so you can stay on one voice at a time. These are the same 167 lines as the production pack, checked against the final 16 songs.

## How to use

1. **Test first.** Before doing everything, make 3 to 5 lines per voice and listen. Lines should be *spoken*, with comic timing, not sung or musical. If the voice feature can only sing, stop there and use ElevenLabs for the voices instead.
2. **Keep each voice the same** for every one of its lines.
3. **Name each file exactly as its clip id**, e.g. `traffic-01-2.mp3`, and drop it in your PC's Downloads folder. Claude checks, trims and wires them in.
4. **Directions** (in italics) say how a line should sound. Use them as a style or emotion hint if the tool takes one; otherwise just pick the take that matches.
