# K-JAM songs: everything for Suno in one place

## Settings for every song (set once)

- **Mode:** Custom
- **Exclude styles:** modern pop, EDM, trap, autotune, lo-fi
- **Duration:** Custom, 2:00 to 2:30
- **Weirdness:** about 30–35%
- **Style Influence:** about 65–75%
- **Variety:** Normal
- **Personalize:** Off
- **Max Mode:** check the ⓘ. If it doesn't cost extra, compare On and Off on one song

## For each song

1. Copy the **Title** and **Style of Music** below into Suno.
2. Set **Vocal Gender**.
3. Paste the **Lyrics** block into the Lyrics box, including the [Verse] and [Chorus] tags. Suno sings exactly what's there.
4. Listen to both versions, download the better one as MP3, and drop it in your PC's Downloads folder. Claude renames and checks it.

The lyrics are original, and every chorus sings the title so the song matches its radio intro.
